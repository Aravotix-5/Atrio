How a project runs

Four steps, and you see the price in writing before any money moves.

  1. Choose your pieces

    Work through the builder and select what your project actually needs.

  2. Send the request

    Tell us what you are making. We read it and come back with questions if anything is unclear.

  3. Get a written quote

    We confirm the scope and the final price. The builder total is only an estimate until this point.

Then you accept and pay. Payment happens on Stripe's secure checkout page, and the project starts once it clears.

How payment works

Card details are entered on Stripe's own checkout page. They never reach Atrio's servers, and no card number, CVV or bank login is ever stored here. Atrio keeps only the amount, the payment status and Stripe's reference ids.

Payment is only unlocked after you have seen and accepted a written quote.

Native apps, described honestly

A responsive website is not an App Store app. Atrio lists them separately: a responsive site, an installable progressive web app, and native iOS or Android apps, which are a much larger piece of work.
